The Maldives

Why I'd want to visit:

To experience the ultimate tropical paradise and escape to a place of pure relaxation. It's a chance to disconnect and enjoy the simple pleasure of doing nothing in one of the world's most beautiful island.

Staying in an overwater bungalow, snorkeling in crystal-clear waters with vibrant marine life like turtles, reef and manta rays, enjoying unique luxuries like dining on a private sandbank.
