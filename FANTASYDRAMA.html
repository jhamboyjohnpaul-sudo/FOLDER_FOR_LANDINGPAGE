ENCANTADIA

MULAWIN

The struggle between the bird-human race of Mulawin and the rebellious Ravena, where destiny depends on the chosen one.

Released: 2004

DARNA

Narda discovers her hidden legacy as the Earth's protector, Darna, as she fights extraterrestrial threats to humanity.

Directors: Chito S. Roño, et al.

Released: 2022

Casts: Jane de Leon, Joshua Garcia, Janella Salvador
