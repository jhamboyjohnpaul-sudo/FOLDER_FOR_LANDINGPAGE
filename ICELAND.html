Iceland: Northern Lights

Why I'd want to visit:

To chase one of nature's most spectacular shows. Seeing the Aurora Borealis is a magical, giving once in a lifetime experience, where the dark winter sky can erupt in dancing curtains of green, pink, and purple light.

I'd love to join a guided hunt for the lights, learning the folklore and science behind them, and enjoying the cozy Icelandic traditions of hot chocolate and warm blankets while waiting under the stars.
