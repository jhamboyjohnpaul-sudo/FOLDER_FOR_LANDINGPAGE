Pandemic Movies

Thrilling stories of virus outbreaks and humanity's fight for survival.

BIRD BOX

Five years after an ominous unseen presence drives most of society to suicide, a survivor and her two children make a desperate bid to reach safety.

Director: Susanne Bier

Released: 2018

Duration: 124min

Casts: Sandra Bullock, Trevante Rhodes

WORLD WAR Z

Former United Nations investigator Gerry Lane travels the world in a race against time to stop a zombie pandemic that is toppling armies and governments.

Director: Marc Forster

Released: 2013

Duration: 116min

Casts: Brad Pitt, Mireille Enos

I AM LEGEND

Years after a plague kills most of humanity and transforms the rest into monsters, the sole survivor in New York City struggles to find a cure.

Director: Francis Lawrence

Released: 2007

Duration: 101min

Casts: Will Smith, Alice Braga
