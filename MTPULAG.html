Mt. Pulag, Benguet

Why I'd want to visit:

To witness a world-famous natural phenomenon and stand above the clouds. Known as the "Playground of the Gods," it's the highest peak in Luzon, offering a once-in-a-lifetime sunrise view over a vast sea of clouds.

I want to do trekking through unique ecosystems (pine forests, mystical mossy forests, grassland summit) to catch the sunrise from the peak, with spectacular views of the Cordillera mountain range.
