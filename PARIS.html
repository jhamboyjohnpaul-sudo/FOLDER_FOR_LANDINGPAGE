Paris, France

Why I'd want to visit:

To see the world's most iconic monument up close and fall in love with the romance of Paris. It's a symbol of elegance and progress, and a must-see for any visitor to the City of Light.

I would love to ascend the Eiffel Tower for the breathtaking panoramic views of Paris. Having dining at one of its wonderful restaurants would be a memorable experience. I'd stay to watch the monument sparkle with thousands of lights after sunset to end the night in a magical way.
