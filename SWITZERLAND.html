Switzerland

Why I'd want to visit:

To immerse myself in picture-perfect alpine landscapes. It's a destination that offers both thrilling adventure and serene relaxation, from its charming villages to its breathtaking mountain scenery.

I'm eager to hike through wildflower-dotted trails, ski with the Matterhorn as a backdrop, ride the scenic mountain trains that unfold spectacular views right outside the window, and indulge in world-famous cheese and chocolate.
