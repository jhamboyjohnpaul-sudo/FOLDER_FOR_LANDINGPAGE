Switzerland

Why I'd want to visit:

To immerse myself in picture perfect alpine landscapes. It's a destination that offers both thrilling adventure and serene relaxation, from its charming villages to its breathtaking mountain scenery.

I'm eager to Hike through wildflower-dotted trails, skiing with the Matterhorn as a backdrop, riding the train to see the scenic mountain landscapes unfold right outside the window, and indulging in world-famous cheese and chocolate.
